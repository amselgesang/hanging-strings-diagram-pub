Slide (D20): drag a string sideways and its hanger slides freely along the rod — it stops exactly where you drop it and no other string moves (slot reorder retired; same rule as the ring's D17). Click a group swatch to bring that whole group to the front — that and Reset are what re-arrange. Arc / Wave (v2.5 M2–M3): the same rail bent into a gentle sag or sine — strings still hang plumb and their length is still the exact value (D1); knob heights just stop lining up, so read the thread knots. Slid positions carry over between all three planar rails. v2 (D16): strings with a +/− knob have children — press the knob itself to unfurl a sub-rail hanging from it; press − to collapse. The branch hangs from the parent's knob and follows it; children slide along their sub-rail the same positional way.

Strings are placed around the rail ellipse and depth-scaled/faded toward the back (D3's cheap fake-3D), growing/fading continuously as they swing. Slide (D17): drag a string sideways and its hanger slides along the rod — it stays threaded on the rail and stops exactly where you drop it; no other string moves.
